September 2026

Internship — AirNav Indonesia, Information Technology Division

  • Onboarding
  • Website Development
  • Database
  • INMC Dashboard
  • SWORD UI

During my internship at AirNav Indonesia in the Information Technology Division, I participated in various activities related to workplace orientation, website development, and dashboard design and development. The internship began with an onboarding and company orientation program to gain an understanding of the work environment, organizational functions, and the overall internship program.

In the initial stage, I learned the fundamentals of website development using HTML, CSS, and PHP, including structuring web pages, designing user interfaces, and connecting websites to databases. I also practiced developing websites and created a portfolio website to improve my skills in web-based application development.

I was subsequently involved in the development of the INMC (Indonesia Network Management Center) dashboard, starting with team discussions regarding system requirements, features, and the information to be displayed. I also prepared and revised a work plan to serve as a guideline for the dashboard development process, followed by reviewing the data, features, and interface to ensure their alignment with the defined requirements.

The following activities focused on the user interface (UI) development of the SWORD dashboard, including organizing the page structure, arranging interface components, adapting the design based on the provided website reference, and refining the overall appearance to make the dashboard more structured, informative, and user-friendly.

Through these activities, I gained practical experience in website development, project planning, feature validation, and dashboard interface design. The internship also strengthened my technical skills and provided valuable insight into teamwork and the application development process within a professional work environment.
