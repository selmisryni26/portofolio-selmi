21 Sep – 1 Oct 2026

Internship Onboarding — AirNav Indonesia

  • Onboarding
  • Website Development
  • Database
  • Dashboard Planning

The internship began with the onboarding of the Kemnaker 2026 apprentices at the AirNav Indonesia Head Office in Tangerang, which introduced the company and its role in air navigation services that support flight safety. The programme continued with an introduction to the company, the working environment, and the Information Technology division, along with self-introductions among the apprentices so we could learn about one another's backgrounds, interests, and the areas each of us wanted to develop.

Participants then attended the orientation and Kick-Off of the Graduate Apprenticeship Programme, where we received direction on how the internship would be carried out. Once the introductory sessions were finished, the activities moved on to learning and practising website development — from understanding page structure, working with code, and setting up layouts, through to learning how databases are used and how they connect to a web application. The website work also covered adding components, adjusting the layout, checking functionality, and fixing the parts that were not yet correct.

Activities were off on 26 and 27 September. After the break, work resumed with website development and checking, along with adjustments to the layout and functionality of the pages. On 30 September a workplan was prepared for developing the INMC dashboard by identifying the information to be displayed, such as weather information, demand & capacity, volcanic ash advisory, CNSD facility, safety occurrence, air space reservation, and special attention. On 1 October the day started with the Hari Kesaktian Pancasila ceremony and then continued with work on the portfolio website.

What I took from it: experience of a real working environment, the website development process, the use of databases, and how to plan dashboard development — as well as an appreciation of how important it is to check and adjust things step by step so that the work matches what is actually needed.
